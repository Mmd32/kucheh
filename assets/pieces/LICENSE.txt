Chess piece set
===============

The SVG files in this folder are the "Cburnett" chess piece set by Colin M.L.
Burnett, used by lichess and many free chess programs.

- Author: Colin M.L. Burnett
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (CC BY-SA 3.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Original set: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces

The files are used unmodified as the app's chess piece artwork.
